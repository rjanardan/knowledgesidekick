What advisory is

Advisory is the offering for when you want a sidekick across the knowledge agenda — not a single report, but ongoing guidance.

The other offerings are focused pieces: maturity evaluation, lifecycle, formats, training, audits, AEO. Advisory is the broader engagement that cuts across them. It is for the people who want guidance on where to invest, what to fix first, how the pieces fit, and how to make the knowledge layer dependable over time.

Advisory is also where the two modes of engagement meet: inception (proactive design) and production (reactive diagnosis and remediation). In both modes, advisory is the work of helping you make good decisions about the knowledge layer — with the practical knowledge of where it fails and how to make it hold up.

Two modes of advisory

  • Inception, proactive. You are planning an agentic system, an LLM product, or a personal assistant, and you want the knowledge layer designed before it fails in production. Advisory here is about the lifecycle, the formats, the maturity starting point, and the practices that will carry the system forward.
  • Production, reactive. The system is live and the problems are showing up — stale knowledge, weak formats, missing updates, no deprecation, poor grounding. Advisory here is about diagnosis, remediation, and turning the reactive fire-fighting into a durable practice.

The same advisory can move between these modes as the system evolves. The point is to have a sidekick who understands the knowledge layer across both — not just a report at one point in time.

What advisory covers

Advisory is scoped to your situation, but it usually touches:

  • Where you stand. A practical read on the knowledge layer — the lifecycle dimensions, the formats, the maturity, the gaps that matter for the systems you are building.
  • Where to invest. What to fix first, what practice to build, what formats to adopt, what to train, what to audit, and how to make the knowledge layer usable by agent engines.
  • How the pieces fit. How the lifecycle, formats, training, audits, AEO, and the wider agentic knowledge agenda fit together for your situation.
  • The bigger picture. The wider agentic knowledge agenda — knowledge as a first-class layer, the lifecycle, the open-source and open-standards layer, and where your company sits in it.

Advisory is scoped to what matters for you. It is practical, not academic, and it is built around the failures that actually show up in agentic systems.
